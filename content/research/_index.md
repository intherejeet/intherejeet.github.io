## Research Overview

My research addresses fundamental challenges in **machine learning security** and **trustworthy artificial intelligence**, with particular emphasis on developing theoretical foundations and practical methodologies for safe and robust AI systems. I investigate systematic approaches to vulnerability assessment, adversarial robustness enhancement, and safety guarantee mechanisms across computer vision and natural language processing domains.

### Current Research Focus

#### Large Language/Multimodal Model Security

I develop formal verification frameworks and automated assessment methodologies for large language and multimodal models. My work encompasses:

- **Automated Red-teaming Frameworks**: Developed comprehensive safety benchmarking methodologies generating 43,000+ successful adversarial examples for systematic evaluation of agentic retrieval-augmented generation systems
- **Inference-time Safety Enhancement**: Research on lightweight mechanisms for real-time safety control in language model inference, addressing fundamental challenges in dynamic safety constraint satisfaction
- **Vulnerability Assessment Frameworks**: Systematic analysis and development of LLM/LMM vulnerability scanning architectures with principled detection mechanisms
- **Distributed Learning with Privacy**: Design of peer-to-peer knowledge exchange frameworks for LLM agent fine-tuning while maintaining training data privacy

#### Trustworthy Multimodal Agentic Systems

I investigate theoretical foundations for enhancing reliability and safety in multimodal agentic frameworks:

- **Trust Architecture Development**: Architected multimodal agentic RAG frameworks with dedicated trust agents, achieving 6.34× safety gains while maintaining state-of-the-art RAG performance
- **Safety Benchmark Creation**: Development of the first comprehensive safety benchmark suite for agentic RAGs with automated red-teaming capabilities
- **Factual Consistency Mechanisms**: Research on information integration, factual consistency, and safety guarantees in complex reasoning tasks

#### Algorithmic Disparity Assessment

I develop computational frameworks for systematic auditing and quantitative assessment of disparities in large language models:

- **Token-Focused Disparity Probing (TFDP)**: Novel methodology for auditing LLM disparities with up to 42× greater token efficiency through targeted token querying and multi-scale semantic alignment
- **Theoretical Foundations**: Establishing mathematical frameworks for bias detection and mitigation strategies that ensure equitable AI system behavior
- **Scalable Assessment Methodologies**: Development of efficient algorithms for large-scale disparity evaluation across diverse populations

#### Adversarial Robustness and Deep Metric Learning

I advance theoretical understanding and practical implementations of adversarial robustness:

- **MDProp Framework**: Developed novel framework improving accuracy and robustness of CNNs and Vision Transformers for multi-distribution inputs in Deep Metric Learning settings
- **State-of-the-art Performance**: Achieved +2.95% Recall@1 improvement under clean conditions and 2.12× robustness enhancement across multi-distribution scenarios
- **Production-scale Applications**: Enhanced NEC Retail Product Recognition Engine with +9.98% Recall@1 improvements

#### Computer Vision Security

I investigate adversarial vulnerabilities and defense mechanisms in practical computer vision systems:

- **Physical-world Attack Methodologies**: Developed novel adversarial makeup attack methods reducing face recognition accuracy by 45%
- **Transferability Enhancement**: Created regularization penalties for generating highly transferable physical adversarial attacks with 1.17-1.97× improved attack potency
- **Brightness Agnostic Attacks**: Developed brightness agnostic adversarial attacks achieving 39.96% higher attack success rates
- **Patch-noise Combo Attacks**: Innovative attack methodologies achieving 2.39-4.74× improvements in attack potency

#### Training Data Attribution and Copyright Protection

I develop attribution methodologies for generative models to address copyright protection concerns:

- **MONTRAGE Framework**: Contributed to unified framework for monitoring training data attribution in generative diffusion models
- **Model Provenance Tracking**: Research on training data influence estimation and automated monitoring frameworks
- **Copyright Protection Mechanisms**: Development of systematic approaches for intellectual property protection in AI-generated content

### Research Methodology

My research methodology combines theoretical analysis with empirical validation:

1. **Theoretical Foundation Development**: Establishing mathematical frameworks and formal verification approaches
2. **Empirical Validation**: Comprehensive experimental evaluation across multiple datasets and real-world scenarios
3. **System Implementation**: Development of practical frameworks and tools for deployment in production environments
4. **Collaborative Verification**: Extensive collaboration with international research institutions for independent validation
