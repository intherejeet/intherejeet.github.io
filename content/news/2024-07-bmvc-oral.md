Delighted to share that our paper "**ATLANTIS: A Framework for Automated Targeted Language-guided Augmentation Training for Robust Image Search**" has been accepted for **oral presentation** at **BMVC 2024**!

With an acceptance rate of less than 3% for oral presentations, this recognition highlights the significance of our work in automated language-guided augmentation for enhancing image search robustness. ATLANTIS represents a unified multimodal framework that bridges the gap between language understanding and visual search.

I'm the first author on this work, collaborating with R Vainshtein, A Zolfi, A Shabtai, J Brokman, O Hofman, K Fumiyoshi, T Kentarou, and H Kojima. Patent pending.
