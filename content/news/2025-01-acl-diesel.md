Thrilled to announce that our paper "**DIESEL: A Lightweight Inference-Time Safety Enhancement for Language Models**" has been accepted at **ACL 2025** - the premier conference in Natural Language Processing!

DIESEL provides a lightweight framework for enhancing LLM safety at inference time through dynamic token re-ranking based on semantic similarity to negative/adversarial concepts. This work represents a significant step towards making LLMs safer without compromising performance.

This is a collaborative effort with B Ganon, A Zolfi, O Hofman, H Kojima, Y Elovici, and A Shabtai.
