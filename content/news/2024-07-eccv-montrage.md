Excited to announce that our paper "**MONTRAGE: Monitoring Training for Attribution of Generative Diffusion Models**" has been accepted at **ECCV 2024** - one of the top-tier conferences in computer vision!

MONTRAGE addresses critical challenges in text-to-image diffusion models by introducing a framework for monitoring and attributing training data, enabling copyright protection and data provenance tracking. This work is particularly timely given the rapid adoption of generative AI and the associated intellectual property concerns.

This collaborative effort includes J Brokman, O Hofman, R Vainshtein, A Giloni, T Shimizu, O Rachmil, A Zolfi, A Shabtai, Y Unno, and H Kojima. Patent filed.
