Excited to share that our paper "**Insights and Current Gaps in Open-Source LLM Vulnerability Scanners: A Comparative Analysis**" has been accepted at **ACM ICSE RAIE (Research Advances in AI Engineering)** 2024! 

This work provides a comprehensive review and comparative analysis of existing open-source LLM vulnerability scanning frameworks, identifying key gaps and proposing improvements for LLM safety assessment.

Co-authored with J Brokman, O Hofman, O Rachmil, PRS Aishvariya, V Pahuja, A Giloni, R Vainshtein, and H Kojima.
