  <div class="publication-abstract">
    <details>
      <summary>Abstract</summary>
      <p>
      Text-to-image diffusion models have revolutionized content creation but raise significant concerns about copyright infringement and data provenance. MONTRAGE addresses these challenges by introducing a novel framework for monitoring and attributing training data in generative diffusion models. Our approach enables tracking of data usage throughout the training process, providing crucial capabilities for copyright protection and ensuring responsible AI development. We demonstrate the effectiveness of MONTRAGE across various diffusion architectures, showing that it can reliably identify training data contributions while maintaining model performance. This work represents a significant step towards more transparent and accountable generative AI systems.
      </p>
