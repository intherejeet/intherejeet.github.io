## Professional Background

I am a Senior Researcher specializing in AI Security at Fujitsu Research of Europe, with close to 6 years of experience developing cutting-edge methodologies for machine learning security and trustworthy artificial intelligence. My research addresses fundamental theoretical and practical challenges in large language/multimodal model safety, multimodal retrieval-augmented generation systems, and adversarial robustness across computer vision and natural language processing domains.

### Current Position: Fujitsu Research of Europe (July 2023 - Present)

**Senior Researcher | AI Security**

I lead multiple research projects focusing on trustworthy AI systems and machine learning security:

- **Trustworthy Multimodal Agentic RAG**: Leading development of multimodal agentic RAG framework with dedicated trust agents, achieving 6.34× safety gains while maintaining state-of-the-art performance. Developed comprehensive safety benchmark suite with 43,000+ successful adversarial examples.

- **LLM Disparity Assessment**: Developed Token-Focused Disparity Probing (TFDP) methodology for auditing LLM disparities with 42× improved token efficiency through targeted querying and multi-scale semantic alignment.

- **LLM/LMM Security Frameworks**: Developing comprehensive vulnerability scanner and guardrail solution frameworks for LLM-based applications, including formal verification approaches and automated assessment methodologies.

- **Multimodal AI Robustness**: Led ATLANTIS framework development for automated, targeted, language-guided augmentation training, published in BMVC 2024 (Oral track, <3% acceptance rate).

## Research Philosophy

My research is guided by the principle that trustworthy AI systems require rigorous theoretical foundations combined with practical validation. I believe in:

1. **Theoretical Rigor**: Establishing mathematical frameworks and formal verification approaches for AI safety
2. **Empirical Validation**: Comprehensive experimental evaluation across diverse scenarios and datasets
3. **Practical Impact**: Developing methodologies that can be deployed in real-world, safety-critical applications
4. **Collaborative Science**: Engaging with the international research community for independent validation and knowledge exchange
5. **Ethical Responsibility**: Ensuring that AI security research contributes to equitable and beneficial AI deployment
